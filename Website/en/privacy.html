Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 27, 2026

Resonance is designed to run entirely offline. This page explains simply and honestly what the app does — and more importantly, what it doesn't do — with your data.

No data collection

Resonance does not collect any personal data on its own. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising trackers, and no third-party tracking services. The only thing that leaves your device is a message you choose to send from "Contact us" (see below).

Where is your data stored?

Your campaigns, scenes, and audio track library are stored locally, on your own device only. They are never sent to a server.

Messages sent from "Contact us"

From Settings, you can report a problem or suggest an idea without creating an account. Only what you choose to send leaves your device:

  • your message (type, summary, details) and any screenshots you add;
  • if the box is ticked, technical information you can review before sending: app version, device model and system, language and the last lines of the app's error log;

The message goes through a small server hosted by Cloudflare, which stores it in a private space on GitHub that only the developer can read. To sort it, it is also analysed by an AI (Claude, by Anthropic). No email address or other contact detail is asked for. To have a sent message deleted, see Delete your data.

User account

Resonance doesn't require creating an account or signing in.

Contact

For any question about this privacy policy, you can write to narfedome.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Resonance were to introduce an online feature (sync, automatic updates, etc.), this page would be updated accordingly before that feature ships.
