Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 21, 2026

Resonance is designed to run entirely offline. This page explains simply and honestly what the app does — and more importantly, what it doesn't do — with your data.

No data collection

Resonance does not collect, transmit, or share any personal data. The app contains no analytics tools, no advertising trackers, and no third-party tracking services.

Where is your data stored?

Your campaigns, scenes, and audio track library are stored locally, on your own device only. Nothing is sent to a server: Resonance doesn't even have one.

User account

Resonance doesn't require creating an account or signing in.

Contact

For any question about this privacy policy, you can write to narfedome.support@gmail.com.

Changes to this policy

If a future version of Resonance were to introduce an online feature (sync, automatic updates, etc.), this page would be updated accordingly before that feature ships.
