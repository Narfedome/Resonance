Delete your data

Last updated: November 28, 2026

This page explains how to request deletion of data related to Resonance (developer: Narfedome).

Data on your device

Everything you create in Resonance stays on your device and is never sent to a server. To delete it, uninstall the app or clear its data (Android: Settings › Apps › Resonance › Storage › Clear data). The developer has no copy of it.

Messages sent from "Contact us"

Only messages you chose to send from Settings › "Contact us" are kept by the developer. To have them deleted:

  1. email narfedome.support@gmail.com with the subject "Delete my data - Resonance";
  2. give the approximate date you sent it and the summary (one-sentence line) of your message: since no account or identifier is asked for, this is how it can be found;
  3. you get a confirmation once it has been deleted, within 30 days at the latest.

What is deleted

  • the message (type, summary, details);
  • attached screenshots;
  • attached technical information (app version, device model and system, language, last lines of the error log).

What is kept, and for how long

  • nothing after your request: no copy of the message is kept;
  • without a request, a message is kept while the problem is being handled, then at most 12 months after it is closed;
  • temporary technical data, without the message content, is erased automatically: a per-IP send counter (1 hour) and the message's random identifier, used to avoid duplicates (30 days).
