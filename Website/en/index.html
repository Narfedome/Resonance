Features

Today, DM Tools focuses on the essentials: the sound of your TTRPG campaign.

🎚️

Audio mixer

Layer multiple audio tracks live to set the mood instantly.

📼

Audio library

Centralize your mp3 tracks, reusable from one campaign to the next.

🗂️

Campaign organization

Group your scenes by campaign so you can find them quickly mid-session.

🔒

100% offline

No internet connection required: everything stays stored locally, on your device.
